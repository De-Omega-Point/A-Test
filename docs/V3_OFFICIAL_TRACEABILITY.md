## Exam-critical precision and editorial watchpoints
- The **2020 booklet**, not generic outside material, is the authority for study assertions.
- Do not conflate the **First Fleet's arrival in 1788** with Federation in **1901**.
- For a federal constitutional referendum, the double majority means **a majority of voters nationally and a majority in at least four of the six states**; territory votes count towards the national result, not the state count.
- The booklet names **King Charles III** as Australia's Head of State, and the **Governor-General** as the King's representative; the **Prime Minister** leads the Australian Government.
- A **Welcome to Country** and an **Acknowledgement of Country** are different protocols.
- Voting is compulsory for eligible citizens in federal and state/territory elections and referendums; local-government obligations vary by jurisdiction.
- The app is an **independent practice tool**, not the official citizenship examination.
- Do not present additional teaching scenarios as verbatim official questions or claim statistical representativeness of the practice question bank.
